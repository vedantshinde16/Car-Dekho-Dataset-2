This project presents a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of the CarDekho dataset, aimed at uncovering key insights into the used car market and identifying factors that influence vehicle pricing and sales trends. The dataset consists of approximately 15,000+ records with a mix of numerical and categorical features related to car specifications, ownership, and pricing.

 Project Objectives
To analyze the structure and distribution of the dataset.
To identify key factors affecting car selling prices.
To detect patterns, trends, and anomalies in vehicle data.
To provide data-driven insights for pricing and business decision-making.
 Data Understanding
Dataset contains 14 features including car name, brand, model, vehicle age, fuel type, transmission, and selling price.
Includes both numerical and categorical variables.
Initial data inspection performed to assess data quality, missing values, and feature distributions.
 Data Preprocessing
Cleaned and standardized column names.
Checked for missing values and inconsistencies.
Converted categorical variables into meaningful formats where required.
Created derived features such as vehicle age groups for better segmentation.
 Exploratory Analysis
Univariate analysis conducted to understand individual feature distributions.
Bivariate and multivariate analysis performed to explore relationships between variables.
Visualization techniques used include bar plots, histograms, scatter plots, box plots, and pie charts.
Identified dominant brands (e.g., Maruti, Hyundai) and popular models in the dataset.
  Key Insights
Vehicle age plays a significant role in determining selling price.
Manual transmission cars dominate the dataset compared to automatic.
Certain brands hold a larger market share and influence pricing trends.
Older vehicles still retain value depending on condition and brand reliability.
 Tools & Technologies
Python (Pandas, NumPy)
Data Visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn)
Jupyter Notebook
 Conclusion

This EDA project provides a strong foundation for further predictive modeling tasks such as price prediction. The insights derived can help stakeholders understand market dynamics, optimize pricing strategies, and make informed business decisions.
